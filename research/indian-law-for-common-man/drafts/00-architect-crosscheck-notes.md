# Architect cross-check notes (independent verification, 5 Sep 2026)

- Central Acts in force: 859 (845 Acts + 14 Spent Acts), excluding Finance Acts.
  Source: Wikipedia "List of acts of the Parliament of India" (current as of June 2026),
  citing Ministry of Law & Justice / India Code.
  Legislative Dept alphabetical list PDF: cdnbbsr.s3waas.gov.in/.../202407091369269213.pdf
- Constitutional amendments enacted: 106 (106th = Nari Shakti Vandan Adhiniyam, 2023).
- Constitution (131st Amendment) Bill, 2026: introduced LS 16 Apr 2026; DEFEATED after 21-hr debate,
  298 for / 230 against of 528 (needed 352). Delimitation Bill 2026 and UT Laws (Amendment) Bill 2026 withdrawn.
  Sources: PRS billtrack, LiveLaw, Outlook, Drishti.
- Constitution (130th Amendment) Bill, 2025 (removal of ministers detained 30 days): introduced 20 Aug 2025,
  referred to Joint Committee; not enacted as of Sep 2026 (PRS).
- Note: indiacode.nic.in and wikipedia are egress-blocked in this sandbox; WebSearch snippets used.
- Repealing and Amending Act, 2025: assent 20 Dec 2025; repeals 71 obsolete Acts (1886-2023); govt claims 1,500+
  central laws repealed since 2014 (Drishti, PRS, SCC Online blog 21 Jan 2026).
- State laws scale: Maharashtra alone has "over a thousand laws in effect" (Vidhi); Maharashtra Repealing Bill 2026
  scrapped 80 obsolete laws (News on AIR, 18 Mar 2026). Govt identified ~1,800 laws (central+state) for removal.
